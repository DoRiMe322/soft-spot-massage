Your visit

What happens in the hour

The same calm order every time, so you can switch off from the moment you arrive.

First

A short chat

Five minutes on what hurts, any injuries and the pressure you like. Then the room is yours to get ready.

Then

The massage

Warm oil, a heated table and covered with a towel throughout. Speak up any time; silence is fine too.

After

Tea and rest

A cup of herbal tea and a few quiet minutes before you head back out. Pay by card or cash.

Questions

Before your first visit

Anything else, write to the studio. Mira answers within a day.

Email a question
What should I wear?

Whatever you arrive in. You undress only as far as you are comfortable, and you stay covered by a towel throughout.

Can I cancel or move my session?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before. After that, send a message and we will sort it out.

Can I have a massage when pregnant or unwell?

Say so when you request your session, so Mira can check it is suitable. If you are unsure, ask your doctor first.

How often should I come?

Many people come every three to four weeks. For a specific problem, two or three visits closer together usually help more.

Will deep tissue hurt?

It is firm, and some tenderness the next day is normal. It should never be sharp pain. Say so and the pressure changes straight away.

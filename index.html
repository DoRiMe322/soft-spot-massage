Questions about a session

What people ask before their first visit. Email a question

What should I wear for a massage?

Whatever you arrive in. You undress only as far as you’re comfortable, and you stay covered by a towel throughout.

Can I cancel or move my massage?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before. After that, send a message and we’ll sort it out.

Can I have a massage when pregnant or unwell?

Say so when you request your session, so Mira can check it’s suitable. If you’re unsure, ask your doctor first.

How often should I book a session?

Many people come every three to four weeks. For a specific problem, two or three visits closer together.
